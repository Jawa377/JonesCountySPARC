SPARC Textbook Studio

An authoring tool that turns SPARC Academy teachers' own materials into standards-aligned textbook sections, assignments, and quizzes. The teacher stays the author: every word is editable, and nothing publishes on its own.

Every element carries a Georgia standard retrieved from the state's published CASE data, never recalled by a model. The coverage panel reports gaps honestly, because a tool that always claims perfect coverage is a tool no teacher trusts.

Phase 1

The Authoring Studio and the Standards Engine, for one pilot unit: 6th Grade Science, Unit 3, "Water in Earth's Systems." Phase 1 touches no student data. Draft generation is not connected yet.
