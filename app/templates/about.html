SPARC Textbook Studio

An authoring tool for SPARC Academy teachers. A teacher adds materials they already wrote, and the studio drafts a textbook section, assignments, and a quiz. Every element is tagged to a Georgia standard retrieved from the state's published CASE data, never generated.

Phase 1 covers the Authoring Studio and the Standards Engine for one pilot unit: 6th Grade Science, Unit 3, "Water in Earth's Systems." It touches no student data. Draft generation is not connected yet.

A partnership between the Jones County School System and Georgia College & State University.
